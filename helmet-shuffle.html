Raider Arena

Open this game in Edge or Chrome. Use the Game hub to choose another game. You can also download the entire project and open index.html for offline play. Use extended displays (Win+P), move the board to your stadium output, and open the operator window on your control monitor. Press F and H on the board for a clean fullscreen display.

Space starts, reveals, or starts the next round. 1 / 2 / 3 picks a cup, helmet, or race lane. In Memory Match, type 1-12 then Enter, or click a card. P pauses, R resets, O opens the operator window, H hides controls, and ? opens help. Backspace clears a typed card number.

Tuning: Presets and sliders apply to the current game, between rounds. Cups and helmets use faster continuous motion, varying swap times, and three-way moves at higher settings. The hidden ball always follows the same physical object. Race winners are random and equally likely; race pace changes duration, not winning odds.

Shuffle times vary and accelerate during the round. Impossible is a novelty challenge, with extremely fast motion. Memory always has six pairs; its clock begins at Start, and higher presets shorten the time and mismatch display. Matched cards stay visible. At zero, inputs lock and unmatched cards are revealed.

Switching games or resetting a running round asks for confirmation. Minimizing the board or hiding its browser tab pauses play; moving focus to the operator window does not. Sounds are optional and off by default. Rehearse fullscreen and the operator window on your actual video-board system before the event.
